League Points System

Every sale and achievement contributes to your League Points, which are earned automatically for each sale and performance metric you achieve.

D2D Points Breakdown

  • Fiber Orders → 1 Point per order
  • Non-Fiber Attachments Sold → 1 Point per item
  • Holiday Acceleration (Special Bonus) → 3 Points per order
  • Recruits Hired (Earn points for onboarding new reps) → 1 Point per recruit’s first 10 orders

MDU Points Breakdown

  • 1GB+ Fiber Orders → 3 Points per order
  • 500MB Fiber Orders (AT&T Only) → 2 Points per order
  • < 1GB Fiber Orders (including all copper orders, excluding 500MB AT&T orders) → 1 Point per order
  • Attachment Orders (TV, Wireless, AT&T Internet Air)→ 2 Points per order

BOOSTERS vs. League Points
Clarification

01
League Points

Points earned all season long on every order to LEVEL UP through the leagues and earn rewards.

02
Boosters

Tangible rewards (cash, swag, trips) for winning competitions. Special, short term events where you can earn points, swag, or even cash money.
